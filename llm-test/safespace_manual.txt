## D1 | Private Journals
safespace journals are private. only you can read your journal.

## D2 | Task Rewards
each completed task unlocks a reward like a sticker or a frame.

## D3 | Forums
you must join a forum before you can post or reply.

## D4 | Joint Tasks
a joint task counts for both friends after the invite is accepted.

## D5 | Daily Limit
each task can be completed once per day.

## D6 | Crisis Help
if you feel unsafe call or text nine eight eight.

## D7 | Friends
search for a username and send an invite to add a friend.

## D8 | Other App
